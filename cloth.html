Three useful comparisons

What would change the result?

Flatten the starting cloth.

Set wrap to zero. The image stops widening, though gaps still change which parts leave a mark.

Make the two forms equal.

Raise relief depth to 100%. Both imprints and guide spans should agree. The geometry—not a different face—causes the difference.

Change how marks form.

Switch to contact only. Hollows may leave no mark. A distance rule can fill them, without undoing the cloth’s lateral distortion.

From the literature

A published model.
A different experiment.

The reference face is adapted from Cicero Moraes’s open 3D files. His contact-simulation study compared a body with a low relief. This lab uses a simpler strip model to isolate how cloth geometry changes an image.

It does not score a match to the Shroud or reproduce his fabric simulation. The shallow relief here compresses the same reference head.
